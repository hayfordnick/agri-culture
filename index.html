The problem we solve

Factories near the farms still can’t get enough supply.

Many processors run well below capacity because supply from smallholders is irregular. Meanwhile, farmers struggle to find dependable buyers and get paid on time.

Inconsistent volumesSupply swings week to week, so lines sit idle.
Quality gapsProduce arrives the wrong grade, too old, or mixed.
Hard to organiseThousands of small farmers, no single point of contact.
Side-sellingAgreed supply goes to whoever pays first.

How it works

From your requirements to a supplier you can rely on.
